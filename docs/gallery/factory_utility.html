Plant meeting | Fresh steam per tonne

Summary

Fresh steam per tonne rose from 111.11 kg/t on January 1 to 307.14 kg/t on January 2 (utility_batches.xlsx, both sheets). The two-day comparison does not establish a loss of efficiency.

January 1 pooled rate

111.11 kg/t

Fresh steam in kg divided by output in tonnes across 5 assigned records, including any shutdown at 0 kg and 0 t.

January 2 pooled rate

307.14 kg/t

Fresh steam in kg divided by output in tonnes across 4 assigned records, including any shutdown at 0 kg and 0 t.

January 1 pooled minus simple

-38.89 kg/t

Difference of the two January 1 rates in kg/t. This subtraction has no numerator or denominator.

January 2 pooled minus simple

82.14 kg/t

Difference of the two January 2 rates in kg/t. This subtraction has no numerator or denominator.

  • Product H stayed at 400 kg/t and product L stayed at 75 kg/t, while H's share of output rose from 11.11% to 71.43%. (utility_batches.xlsx, batches rows 3–5 and 7–11, meter_readings rows 3–6 and 8–12)
  • The shift in output shares accounts arithmetically for the full 196.03 kg/t increase in the pooled rate. (Product-level totals and output shares from both workbook sheets)

Assumptions & limitations

  • The workbook covers recorded batch-associated fresh steam only. There are two dates and gaps between recorded batch intervals. The data do not measure plant-wide steam outside the recorded intervals or establish comparable operating conditions.
  • No blanks or parsing failures occur in the relevant source data. After excluding the one exact production duplicate, there are no duplicate batch IDs or reading IDs. Meter readings have one deliberately repeated batch ID.
  • The 9.5 t, 3,800 kg product H batch on January 2 is the largest observed batch and remains in scope. Its rate equals the other product H batch rates.
  • Individual points show the distribution directly with eight batches, so a histogram is omitted. A continuous time chart would imply coverage through gaps in the records.

# Contents of analyze.py
import json
from pathlib import Path

import matplotlib
matplotlib.use("Agg")
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import pandas as pd


data = pd.read_csv("delivery/cleaned_utility_batches.csv", dtype={"batch_id": str, "reading_id": str})
batches = data.loc[data["_source_sheet"].eq("batches"), [
    "_source_row", "batch_id", "product", "start_at", "end_at", "status", "production", "production_unit"
]].rename(columns={"_source_row": "batch_row"}).copy()
meter = data.loc[data["_source_sheet"].eq("meter_readings"), [
    "_source_row", "reading_id", "batch_id", "reading_at", "steam_kg"
]].rename(columns={"_source_row": "meter_row"}).copy()
for frame in (batches, meter):
    assert frame.notna().all().all()
    assert not frame.drop(columns=frame.columns[0]).duplicated().any()
assert batches["batch_id"].is_unique and meter["reading_id"].is_unique
assert set(batches["batch_id"]) == set(meter["batch_id"])
assert set(batches["production_unit"]) == {"kg", "t"}
batches["output_t"] = pd.to_numeric(batches["production"]) * batches["production_unit"].map({"kg": 0.001, "t": 1})
batches["start_at"] = pd.to_datetime(batches["start_at"]).dt.tz_localize("Asia/Seoul")
batches["end_at"] = pd.to_datetime(batches["end_at"]).dt.tz_localize("Asia/Seoul")
meter["reading_at"] = pd.to_datetime(meter["reading_at"]).dt.tz_localize("Asia/Seoul")
meter["steam_kg"] = pd.to_numeric(meter["steam_kg"])
assert batches["output_t"].ge(0).all() and meter["steam_kg"].ge(0).all()
assert len(meter.merge(batches[["batch_id"]], on="batch_id", validate="many_to_one")) == len(meter)
by_batch = meter.groupby("batch_id", as_index=False).agg(steam_kg=("steam_kg", "sum"), meter_rows=("meter_row", list))
joined = batches.merge(by_batch, on="batch_id", validate="one_to_one")
assert len(joined) == len(batches) == len(by_batch)
assert joined["steam_kg"].sum() == meter["steam_kg"].sum()
assert joined["output_t"].sum() == batches["output_t"].sum()
assert sum(map(len, joined["meter_rows"])) == len(meter)
joined["day"] = joined["start_at"].dt.strftime("%Y-%m-%d")
joined["rate"] = joined["steam_kg"].div(joined["output_t"].where(joined["output_t"].gt(0)))
running = joined.loc[joined["output_t"].gt(0)]
assert running["status"].eq("running").all()
running.to_csv("batch_rates.csv", index=False)
daily = joined.groupby("day").agg(steam_kg=("steam_kg", "sum"), output_t=("output_t", "sum"),
                                  rate_sum=("rate", "sum"), n_rates=("rate", "count"))
daily["pooled"] = daily["steam_kg"] / daily["output_t"]
daily["simple"] = daily["rate_sum"] / daily["n_rates"]
daily["gap"] = daily["pooled"] - daily["simple"]
product = running.groupby(["day", "product"]).agg(steam_kg=("steam_kg", "sum"),
                                                   output_t=("output_t", "sum"), batches=("rate", "count"))
product["pooled"] = product["steam_kg"] / product["output_t"]
product["output_share"] = product["output_t"] / product.groupby(level="day")["output_t"].transform("sum")
print(joined[["batch_row", "meter_rows", "product", "day", "output_t", "steam_kg", "rate"]].to_string(index=False))
print(daily.to_string())
print(product.to_string())

results = {
    "daily": daily.reset_index().to_dict("records"),
    "product": product.reset_index().to_dict("records"),
    "records": json.loads(joined[["batch_row", "meter_rows", "day", "product", "output_t", "steam_kg", "rate"]].to_json(orient="records")),
}
Path("results.json").write_text(json.dumps(results, indent=2))

fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(7.5, 4.2), layout="constrained")
figure_data = daily[["pooled", "simple"]].rename(columns={"pooled": "Total steam ÷ total output", "simple": "Simple batch average"})
figure_data.index = ["January 1", "January 2"]
figure_data.plot.bar(ax=ax, color=["#126A73", "#C76135"], width=0.58, rot=0)
for bars in ax.containers:
    ax.bar_label(bars, fmt="%.2f", padding=4)
ax.set(ylim=(0, 365), xlabel="Batch start date (KST)", ylabel="Fresh steam (kg/t)",
       title="Daily fresh-steam intensity by calculation")
ax.legend(frameon=False, loc="upper left")
ax.spines[["top", "right"]].set_visible(False)
fig.savefig("delivery/figures/daily_rates.png", dpi=160)
